4. LINES PRESERVING THE CHIRAL ALGEBRAS (alpha-induction, simple-current formulas)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Classification [literature] (Boeckenhauer-Evans-Kawahigashi; Fuchs-Runkel-Schweigert): for a simple-current
extension/orbifold every Verlinde line a of the product gives alpha^+(a) and alpha^-(a); alpha^+(a) = alpha^-(a)
(a genuine Verlinde line of the extended theory) iff a is local with all currents.  One of alpha^+/alpha^-
preserves the left, the other the right extended algebra; each fuses like the product: alpha(a)alpha(b) = alpha(ab).

only with orbifold currents come as alpha^+/alpha^- pairs differing by a quantum-symmetry line (section 1).
